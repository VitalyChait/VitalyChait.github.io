POLYGAINS

Prediction-market intelligence from market and wallet activity.

PolyGains is a market-analysis project that organizes decentralized prediction-market activity into useful views of orders, transactions, wallets, and trading patterns.

MARKET DATA

Activity across the market

The project tracks order-book changes and transaction flows to help explain how a market develops over time.

WALLET ANALYSIS

Behavior and clustering

Wallet activity can reveal recurring behavior, connected strategies, and concentrations of market participation.

ENGINEERING

Real-time analytical workflows

The work combines data extraction, transaction processing, heuristic clustering, and interfaces for inspecting market behavior.
